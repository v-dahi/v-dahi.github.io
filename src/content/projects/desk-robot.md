## Why I'm Building It

Most of my experience is on the electronics and sensing side, so I started this personal project to push into the areas I'm least comfortable with: mechanical design, CAD, and physical build.

## What I'm Doing

Teaching myself Fusion 360 CAD from scratch to model the robot's body, my first real mechanical design work, and 3D-printing the parts. I'm owning every design decision about how it looks, moves, and fits together, learning the tools as I go rather than waiting until I already knew them.

## What I'm Learning

This is the project I've grown the most on, precisely because so much of it was unfamiliar when I started. It's teaching me the mechanical and fabrication side that complements my electronics background.
