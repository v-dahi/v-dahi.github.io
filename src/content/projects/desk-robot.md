## Why I'm building it
Most of my experience is on the electronics and sensing side, so I started this personal project to push into the areas I'm least comfortable with: mechanical design, CAD, and physical build.

## What I'm doing
- Teaching myself Fusion 360 CAD from scratch to model the robot's body — my first real mechanical design work — and 3D-printing the parts.
- Owning every design decision about how it looks, moves, and fits together, learning the tools as I go.

## What I'm learning
This is the project I've grown the most on, precisely because so much of it was unfamiliar when I started.
