## Why I'm building it
Most of my experience is on the electronics and sensing side, so I started this
personal project specifically to push into the areas I'm least comfortable with:
mechanical design, CAD, and physical build.

## What I'm doing
- Teaching myself Fusion 360 CAD from scratch to model the robot's body — my
  first real mechanical design work — and 3D-printing the parts.
- Owning every design decision about how it looks, moves, and fits together,
  and learning the tools as I go rather than waiting until I already knew them.

## What I'm learning
This is the project I've grown the most on, precisely because so much of it was
unfamiliar when I started. It's teaching me the mechanical and fabrication side
that complements my electronics background — and how to keep making progress in
territory I don't fully know yet.
